MASSE

Privacy policy

Effective 8 October 2026

MASSE is a music app for iPhone. It collects no personal data.

WHAT MASSE DOES NOT DO

  • There is no account, sign-in or registration.
  • There are no analytics, crash reports, advertising or tracking.
  • MASSE makes no network connections. Everything happens on your iPhone.

WHAT STAYS ON YOUR IPHONE

Your songs, sounds, patterns, samples and recordings are saved inside the app, on your iPhone. If you back up your iPhone (to iCloud or a computer), they are part of that backup, under Apple's terms. Deleting the app deletes them.

MICROPHONE

MASSE uses the microphone only while you record a VOICE sound or a MIC track. The recording is saved on your iPhone and is never sent anywhere. You can turn microphone access off in Settings › MASSE.

MIDI

MIDI devices you connect are used only to play notes and controls inside MASSE.

SHARING

Files leave your iPhone only when you export or share them yourself, for example through AirDrop, Files or Messages. What happens to them after that depends on where you send them.

CHILDREN

MASSE does not collect data from anyone, children included.

CHANGES

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
